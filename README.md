# Project 1: Excel Analysis: Bike Store Sales in Europe
# Project Overview
This is projects involves analysing the Bike Store Sales in Europe dataset from Kaggle. The dataset contains a mix of categorical data (Country, Gender, Product) and numerical data (Revenue, Profit, Cost, Price), which makes it ideal for analysis using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on sales and revenue, customer demographics, geographical performance, product analysis and financial performance of the business. Throughout the analysis, I utilize pivot tables and excel formulas to analyze and derive critical business insights from the bike sales. The primary goal was to perform data cleaning, pivoy tables and dashboard creation using Microsoft Excel.

## Business Problem
The manager wants to determine the most profitable demographic and gender to understand the target audience for his business. The director desires a marketing campaign tailored according to the most profitable product and the geographical locations which generate more sales (revenue) to facilitate the scalability of the business. Moreover, it is crucial to understand the most profitable season, exemplied by peak year and months to know when to do the campaign. The business analysis questions include:
* Which product categories generate the most revenue?
* In which locations does the business make the most sales and least sales?
* Which is the most profitable demographic and season?

### Sales Trends
From the data visualization, the most sales were made in 2015 and the least in 2011. Moreover, most sales happened in the United States of America while Germany generated the least sales. The products that generated most sales are bikes with more than 7 million sales, accessories with at least 3 million sales, and clothing with at least 900,000 sales, respectively. Moreover, the masculine gender generated more revenue than their feminine counterpart. 
### Product Performance 
Bikes performed best among the three product categories as it generated most profit. Accessories came second while the clothing product category trailed with the least sales and profit.
### Customer Segmentation
From the revenue per country and age group visualization, adults between the ages of 35-64 bought the most bikes across the business's six locations (Australia, Canada, France, Germany, United Kingdom, United States).Young adults aged 25-34 generated the second best sales as they bought more bikes than youth (<25) and the seniors (64+). Seniors generated the least sales as they bought less bikes compared to the rest. Moreover, most revenue was generated by the male gender, while the female gender generated less.
## Recommendations
* The business should focus on opening more stores in the United States since the USA generated more profit and revenue for the country than the rest of the business locations. 
* The business should stock more bikes in their six locations since this is the product that is generating more sales, profit and revenue.
* The marketing campaign should focus on pulling more ladies to the business since they are generating considerably less revenue compared to males.
## Caveats and Assumptions
* The dataset used is static, not dynamic. Thus, the data does not change with time.
