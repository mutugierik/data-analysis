# Project 1: Excel Analysis: Bike Store Sales in Europe
# Project Background
This is projects involves analysing the Bike Store Sales in Europe dataset from Kaggle. The dataset contains a mix of categorical data (Country, Gender, Product) and numerical data (Revenue, Profit, Cost, Price), which makes it ideal for analysis using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focus on sales and revenue, customer demographics, geographical performance, product analysis and financial performance of the business. Throughout the analysis, I utilize pivot tables and excel formulas to analyze and derive critical business insights from the bike sales.

## Business Problem
*The manager wants to determine the most profitable demographic and gender to understand the target audience for his business.*
*To know the most profitable product and the geographical locations which generate more sales (revenue).*
*To understand the most profitable season, exemplied by peak year and months.*
